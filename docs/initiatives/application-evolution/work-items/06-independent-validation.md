# AE-06 — Independent validation and disposition

Read the [charter](../README.md); claim and report work in [PROGRESS](../PROGRESS.md).
Dependencies: internal findings from AE-01 through AE-05, a recorded decision selecting pilot
hypotheses, and consenting participants. This card owns the pilot kit and disposition report.

## Outcome and existing evidence

Learn whether people outside the implementation effort can make successful subsequent changes
with less repeated architectural assistance. Target three independent teams, seeking a mix
of experience and agent use; record the actual participant mix and sample size honestly.

Read the prior cards' receipts, the [skill rubric](../../../../evals/koan/rubric.md), and
[publication/privacy requirements](../../announcement/ACCEPTANCE.md). Prepare the kit before
outreach; contacting participants and publishing findings are separate authorized actions.

## Deliver and prove

1. Freeze a runnable baseline, task instructions, accepted-outcome checks, foundation update,
   and worked review report. Include setup requirements and a way to capture failure evidence.
2. Record the hypotheses selected at the internal decision point, assistance policy, time
   budget, stopping conditions, and consent/privacy handling. Keep raw private material out
   of the public tree; public reproductions use synthetic applications and data.
3. Ask participants to complete first use and then a meaningful second change. Include shared
   policy consumption and review where the participant's role makes that comparison useful.
4. Capture accepted changes, time, reviewer effort, interventions, regressions, and qualitative
   friction. Record help supplied, failed attempts, withdrawals, and unavailable measurements.
5. Decide continue, refine, or stop for each tested hypothesis. Transfer actionable defects,
   reusable guidance, and follow-up work to their permanent owners; close or explicitly
   re-scope the initiative using the charter's completion criteria.

## Acceptance and limits

- Findings identify independent observations separately from maintainer and automated runs.
- Every conclusion names its evidence, participant/task limits, and plausible confounding.
- Public material has appropriate consent and reproducible, non-identifying evidence.
- No independent uptake or productivity improvement is claimed without observations supporting it.

If recruitment is unavailable, deliver the ready pilot kit, record the missing evidence, and
decide to defer or close this phase. Do not substitute agent simulations for independent teams.
A small pilot supports product decisions; it does not establish a universal performance claim.
